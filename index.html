    How to Read This Report

    • Completed – a confirmed reservation whose end time has passed.
    • Upcoming – a confirmed reservation that has not finished yet.
    • Cancelled – cancelled by the instructor or the administrator.
    • Hours Booked – total hours of completed and upcoming reservations.
    • Share of Bookings – this classroom's part of all confirmed bookings.
    • Cancellation rate – cancelled reservations divided by all reservations.
